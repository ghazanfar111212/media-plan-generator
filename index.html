Benchmarks and assumptions
These are rough starting points for planning conversations, not guarantees. Real costs vary by industry, season, audience size and creative. Replace them with figures from your own ad accounts. Your edits apply to this session and to share links.

CPM is in USD. CTR is clicks per impression. Conversion rate is the share of clicks that become a lead or sale. Frequency is the average number of times each person sees the ads over the whole flight, used to estimate reach.
